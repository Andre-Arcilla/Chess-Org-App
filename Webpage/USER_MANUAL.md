# Chessistant User Manual

Welcome to the **Chessistant** User Manual. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for navigating and using the club management system, tailored for Administrators, Coaches, and Members.

---

## 1. Accessing the System

This section covers how users access the Chessistant portal.

### Login Process
**Step 1:** Navigate to the Chessistant login portal (`portal.html`).
**Step 2:** Enter your registered **Email Address** and **Password**.
**Step 3:** Click the **Enter Club** button.

> **Note:** If you enter incorrect credentials, a native browser tooltip will appear stating "No account found matching this email address" or "Incorrect password." Please verify your details and try again.

### Registration (For New Members)
**Step 1:** On the login page, click the **"Register here"** link at the bottom.
**Step 2:** Fill in the registration form with your **Full Name**, **Student ID**, **Email**, and a secure **Password**.
**Step 3:** Click **Submit Registration**.

> **Note:** After submission, your application will be marked as "Pending." An Administrator must review and approve your registration before you can log in.

---

## 2. Administrator Guide

Administrators have full control over the club's data, members, and tournaments.

### Managing Registrations
**Step 1:** Log in and navigate to the **Registrations** tab on the sidebar.
**Step 2:** Review the list of pending applications.
**Step 3:** Click **Approve** to grant the user access or **Reject** to dismiss the application.

### Tournament Management
**Step 1:** Navigate to the **Tournaments** section.
**Step 2:** Click **Create Tournament** to start a new event.
**Step 3:** Define the tournament style (e.g., Round Robin), participant count, and description.
**Step 4:** To record results, use the **Tournament Crosstable**. Enter scores (1, 0.5, or 0) directly into the grid; the system will automatically calculate the reciprocal score for the opponent.

### Managing the Organization Roster
**Step 1:** Navigate to **Org Roster**.
**Step 2:** Here you can view and manage the official list of club members, ensuring Student IDs match official records.

---

## 3. Coach Guide

Coaches focus on member progress and tournament oversight.

### Viewing Member Progress
**Step 1:** Access the **Members** tab.
**Step 2:** View student ratings and puzzle progress to identify areas for improvement.

### Posting Announcements
**Step 1:** Navigate to the **Announcements** section.
**Step 2:** Click **New Announcement** to share club news or training schedules.
**Step 3:** Click **Post** to make it visible on the public landing page and member dashboards.

---

## 4. Member Guide

Members can track their own progress and participate in club events.

### My Profile
**Step 1:** Click on **My Profile** in the sidebar.
**Step 2:** View your current **Rating**, **Puzzles Solved**, and account details.

### Participating in Tournaments
**Step 1:** Go to the **Tournaments** tab.
**Step 2:** View upcoming events and your standing in active tournaments.
**Step 3:** Review the **Crosstable** to see your past match results and current ranking.

---

## 5. Troubleshooting & FAQs

**Q: I can't log in even though I just registered.**
*A: Your registration must be approved by an Admin. If it's been more than 48 hours, please contact the club president.*

**Q: The tournament table isn't letting me enter a score.**
*A: Ensure both players are correctly registered for the tournament. Only authorized personnel (Admins/Coaches) can edit scores.*

**Q: How do I change my password?**
*A: Currently, password changes must be requested through a system Administrator.*
